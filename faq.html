Questions and answers

About the stories, and about the tool that publishes them.

The stories

Who are the stories for?

Curious kids and the grown-ups who watch with them. The questions are the everyday ones children ask, and the explanations stay simple.

How long is each story?

Most are under a minute, made to be watched on a phone.

Where can we watch?

On YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

How often do new stories come out?

Twice a day, one in the morning and one in the afternoon.

Can we suggest a question?

Yes, please. Leave it as a comment or send a direct message on any of our channels.

The Publisher

What is Pip & Particle Publisher?

A small scheduling tool we built for ourselves. It posts our finished videos to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook at planned times. See how it works.

Can I use the Publisher for my own account?

Not at the moment. It is a private tool for the Pip & Particle team and has no public sign-up.

Which TikTok permissions does it use?

user.info.basic, video.publish and video.upload, through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. What each one is used for is listed on the Publisher page.

What data does it keep?

Only what it needs to post our own videos: sign-in tokens for our own accounts, the video files and the captions. It stores nothing about viewers. The Privacy Policy has the details.

How is access removed?

In the TikTok app, under Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions. Google and Meta have similar pages for YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. Once access is removed, the stored token stops working.
