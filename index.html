Meet Pip

Pip is a very small particle with very big questions. Why do magnets push? Where does a puddle go? How does a rocket move where there is no air? In every story, Pip goes to find out.

Pip dresses for the job: a lab coat for experiments, a space jacket for the stars and weather gear for storms and rain.

How every story works

Ask

Every episode starts with one real question, the kind kids ask at the dinner table.

Explore
