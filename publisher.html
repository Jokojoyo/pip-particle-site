How it works

Plan

Every video gets a row in our content calendar: the date, a morning or afternoon slot, and a status.

Write

Each platform gets its own title, caption and hashtags, kept in a small text file next to the video. This is the YouTube file for 25 September:

title: How Do Crystals Grow Their Neat Shapes? | Science Story for Kids
tags: crystals, salt, chemistry, geology, patterns, science for kids

Atoms stack in the same repeating pattern over and over, so the flat faces and sharp corners appear all by themselves as the crystal grows.

Try it: look closely at a grain of salt - it is a tiny cube.

#pipandparticle #crystals #salt #shorts

Connect

Each account is connected once through the platform's own sign-in: Sign in with TikTok, Google for YouTube, and Meta for Instagram and Facebook. The Publisher never sees or stores a password.

Publish

At the planned time the Publisher uploads the video through each platform's official API, records the result and flags anything that needs a person. If the computer was off, a late post is held back instead of going out at the wrong hour.

The TikTok integration

The Publisher uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API (Direct Post). It posts only to the TikTok account that signed in, which is the Pip & Particle account.

What the creator decides for every TikTok post

  • Posting accountThe Publisher shows the connected TikTok account, with its display name, before anything is posted.
  • Who can viewChosen from the privacy options TikTok returns for that account.
  • Comments, Duet and StitchEach can be allowed or turned off. Anything disabled in the creator's TikTok settings stays off.
  • Commercial contentThe creator discloses whether a video promotes their own brand, a paid partner, or neither.
  • AI-generated labelApplied when a video contains AI-generated content.
  • Music Usage ConfirmationThe creator agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation before the video is published.

These choices are made per video, either on screen at posting time or ahead of time in the video's post file, and can be changed until the video goes out.

Permissions requested

user.info.basic

Shows which account is connected: display name and profile picture.

video.publish

Posts the finished video directly to the connected account.

video.upload

Included with TikTok's Content Posting API. The Publisher only posts finished videos and does not create drafts.

What it stores

The Publisher runs on our own computer. It keeps the sign-in tokens for our own accounts, our video files and our captions. It collects nothing about viewers or anyone else, and it has no analytics or advertising.

Access can be removed at any time in each platform's app settings. The full details are in our Privacy Policy.

A private tool. The Publisher is used only by the Pip & Particle team. There is no public sign-up, and this website has no login page.
